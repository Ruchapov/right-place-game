====================================================
PROJECT OVERVIEW
====================================================

Game Name:
Right Place

Platform:
Telegram Mini App (Web App)

Genre:
Roguelike with Souls-like combat elements

Initial Mode:
PvE only

Future:
PvP mode planned

====================================================
CAMERA
====================================================

Current preferred option:

2D Side View

Reason:
Combat focuses on fighting a single dangerous enemy.
Dodging is one of the main gameplay mechanics.

====================================================
CORE GAME LOOP
====================================================

Player presses "Start Run"

↓

Selects equipment (or keeps current equipment)

↓

Spends 10 Energy

↓

Receives 3 random rooms

↓

Completes the rooms

↓

Receives rewards

↓

Returns to menu

↓

Upgrades character

↓

Starts another run

====================================================
ENERGY SYSTEM
====================================================

Maximum Energy:
100

Regeneration:
1 Energy per minute

Cost per Run:
10 Energy

====================================================
ROOM SYSTEM
====================================================

Each run consists of 3 random rooms.

Possible room types:

1. Normal Enemy
2. Boss
3. Treasure Chest
4. Trap
5. Smuggler
6. Puzzle

Rooms are completely random.

Possible examples:

Enemy + Enemy + Enemy

or

Chest + Trap + Enemy

or

Boss + Enemy + Puzzle

Boss rooms are not guaranteed.

A player may encounter no boss for many runs.

====================================================
COMBAT SYSTEM
====================================================

Player actions:

- Movement
- Attack
- Dodge
- Skills
- Potion usage

Not included:

- Blocking
- Parrying
- Combo systems

Core combat philosophy:

Attack
→ Dodge
→ Reposition
→ Attack again

====================================================
BASIC ATTACK
====================================================

Starting weapon:

Sword

Attack style:

Single attack

No combo chain

====================================================
DODGE SYSTEM
====================================================

Dodging is skill-based.

Not RNG-based.

Success depends on player timing.

====================================================
SKILLS
====================================================

The player may equip only 2 skills.

There are currently 5 starter skills.

Skill #1

Dash Strike

The player dashes.

If the dash reaches or passes through an enemy, damage is dealt.

--------------------------------

Skill #2

Fireball

Ranged damage projectile.

--------------------------------

Skill #3

Slash

Deals damage.

Applies Bleeding.

Bleeding deals damage every second.

--------------------------------

Skill #4

Heal

Restores health.

--------------------------------

Skill #5

Ice Ball

Deals damage and slows enemies.

--------------------------------

Cooldown:

All skills currently have a 5-second cooldown.

Mana:
None.

Skill resource:
None.

Skills are limited only by cooldowns.

====================================================
HEALING
====================================================

Healing is performed through potions.

Potion uses are limited.

Potions may be used:

- During combat
- Between rooms

====================================================
CHARACTER STATS
====================================================

ENDURANCE

Starting value:
10

Formula:

1 Endurance = 8 HP

Starting Health:
80 HP

Endurance increases based on total damage received over the lifetime of the character.

The more damage received, the more Endurance increases.

--------------------------------

STRENGTH

Increases physical damage.

Strength increases through dealing damage.

--------------------------------

AGILITY

Increases through using skills.

Agility improves skill effectiveness.

--------------------------------

ATTACK SPEED

Determined by weapon.

--------------------------------

CRITICAL CHANCE

Determined by weapon.

--------------------------------

CRITICAL DAMAGE

Starting value:

1.5x

--------------------------------

ARMOR

Provided by equipment.

--------------------------------

ARMOR PENETRATION

Provided by weapons.

--------------------------------

MOVEMENT SPEED

Can be increased through equipment.

Maximum planned value:

1.5x base speed

--------------------------------

LUCK

Affects:

- Gold
- Loot quality
- Rewards

Does NOT affect:

- Accuracy
- Dodge success

====================================================
LEVELING SYSTEM
====================================================

Players can level up in two ways.

METHOD #1

Stat Progression

To gain a level:

Endurance must increase by +3

AND

Strength + Agility combined must increase by +6

Example:

Strength +4
Agility +2

or

Strength +1
Agility +5

--------------------------------

METHOD #2

Boss Kill

Killing a boss immediately grants a level.

Stats do not automatically increase.

====================================================
LEVEL BENEFITS
====================================================

Higher levels increase rewards.

Examples:

- More gold
- More resources
- More trophies

The exact scaling is not yet defined.

====================================================
CURRENCIES
====================================================

GOLD

Primary currency.

Used for character progression and upgrades.

Obtained from:

- Chests
- Events
- Selling items

Normal enemies do NOT drop gold.

--------------------------------

TROPHIES

Main loot currency.

Enemy killed
→ Trophy obtained

Trophies can be exchanged for gold.

If the player dies:

All trophies are lost.

--------------------------------

CRYSTALS

Premium currency.

Obtained through:

- Daily quests
- Real-money purchases

Currently not required for MVP functionality.

====================================================
SMUGGLER
====================================================

The Smuggler is a random room encounter.

The player may exchange all carried trophies.

Trophies are multiplied by 1.5.

Example:

10 trophies → 15 trophies

Core risk/reward concept:

Players are encouraged to hold trophies and risk losing them.

====================================================
EQUIPMENT
====================================================

Equipment slots:

- Weapon
- Helmet
- Armor
- Boots
- Gloves
- Amulet

No rarity system at launch.

Equipment unlocks based on player level.

Weapon:
Damage + additional stats

Helmet:
Defense + additional stats

Armor:
Defense only

Boots:
Movement speed

Gloves:
Not yet defined

Amulet:
Not yet defined

====================================================
ENEMIES
====================================================

Initial enemy faction:

Undead

Enemy Type #1

Melee Fighter

Moves toward the player.

Uses AoE attacks.

--------------------------------

Enemy Type #2

Ranged Fighter

Maintains distance.

Attacks frequently.

====================================================
FIRST BOSS
====================================================

The first boss is an enhanced version of a normal enemy.

Stats:

1.5x normal enemy stats

Abilities:

- AoE attack
- Ranged attack

====================================================
REWARDS
====================================================

Normal Enemy Rewards:

- Trophy
- Chance to drop equipment

Items can only drop at the player's level or below.

--------------------------------

Boss Rewards:

Boss Trophy

Allows the player to choose a permanent stat reward.

Examples:

- Health
- Damage
- Other stats

====================================================
DEATH
====================================================

If the player dies:

The run immediately ends.

Lost:

- All trophies

Kept:

- Character level
- Character stats
- Equipment
- Progression

====================================================
MVP GOAL
====================================================

Primary player objective:

Reach Level 20

Future content unlocks after Level 20, but those systems are not yet designed.
