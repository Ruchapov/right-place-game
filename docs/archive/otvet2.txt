# Additional Design Clarifications

## Level Scaling vs Boss Leveling

Enemies scale with player level.

For every level gained, enemies become stronger by increasing their Health and Damage values.

This means that players who gain levels by defeating bosses but neglect their stat progression will eventually struggle against both normal enemies and future bosses.

Leveling through bosses provides access to better rewards but does not automatically make the character stronger.

---

## Endurance Progression Through Taking Damage

This system is intentional.

Although Endurance increases by receiving damage, players are discouraged from intentionally farming damage because:

* Trophies are the primary progression currency.
* All trophies are lost upon death.
* Runs end immediately when the player dies.

A player repeatedly dying to farm Endurance sacrifices valuable progression resources.

---

## Equipment Progression

New equipment tiers unlock every 5 levels up to Level 50.

Examples:

* Level 5 equipment
* Level 10 equipment
* Level 15 equipment
* Level 20 equipment

Higher-level players also receive more trophies from enemy kills.

This creates a long-term progression curve.

---

## Skill and Enemy Balance

Numerical balancing has not been finalized.

Design goals:

* Combat should remain challenging throughout progression.
* Players should never completely outscale content.
* Skills can be upgraded.
* Skill progression should use diminishing returns.
* Progression should feel meaningful but not allow players to consume all content within a single day.

Exact formulas will be defined during balancing and playtesting.

---

## Smuggler Spawn Rate

Smuggler room chance:

3%

The Smuggler appears as a random room event.

---

## Gloves

Gloves provide progression bonuses.

Different gloves specialize in either:

* Strength Growth
* Agility Growth

The percentage bonus increases with equipment level.

Exact values are not finalized yet.

---

## Amulets

Amulets increase Luck.

Luck bonuses increase with equipment level.

Exact values are not finalized yet.

---

## Strength and Agility Growth

### Strength

Formula:

2 Strength = +1 Damage

Strength is gained through dealing damage.

The exact progression curve should be designed to prevent players from progressing too quickly.

---

### Agility

Agility increases through skill usage.

Each skill benefits from Agility differently.

Examples:

* Increased damage
* Increased effectiveness
* Stronger status effects

Exact scaling will be defined later.

---

## Potions

Starting charges:

3

Healing amount:

50% of maximum Health

Cooldown:

2 seconds

Potions can be used:

* During combat
* Between rooms

Potion charges are restored before a new run if replacement potions are available.

Additional potions can be purchased using Gold.

---

## Endurance Anti-Mastery Effect

The system intentionally remains unchanged.

Although highly skilled players receive less damage and therefore gain Endurance more slowly, Health can also be increased through other progression systems.

---

## Heal Skill

The Heal skill restores:

10% of maximum Health.

It shares the same 5-second cooldown as all other skills.

This is considered acceptable because Heal occupies one of the player's two skill slots and therefore replaces a potential damage skill.

---

## Boss Philosophy

This behavior is intentional.

Bosses are designed to be highly desirable encounters.

Boss rewards:

* Immediate level gain
* Boss Trophy
* Permanent stat reward

Because Boss rooms have a low appearance rate, encountering one should feel exciting and valuable.

---

## Economy Philosophy

This is intentional.

Gold is meant to be less reliable than trophies.

Players are expected to take risks and make decisions regarding trophy management.

Randomness is part of the intended experience.

---

## Smuggler Risk

Smuggler room chance:

3%

Additional risk:

20% chance that the Smuggler steals 50% of the player's carried trophies.

This prevents the Smuggler from becoming a completely safe multiplier.

---

## Progression Speed

The exact progression speed toward Level 20 has not been finalized.

The plan is to build the MVP first, then adjust progression through playtesting.

---

## Different Progression Paths

This behavior is intentional.

Players who focus on:

* Boss hunting
* Stat grinding

will reach Level 20 with different builds and power levels.

Build diversity is considered a positive feature.

---

## Endurance Farming Prevention

Players cannot leave combat safely.

If a player exits a run early:

* The run is considered failed.
* All carried trophies are lost.

This discourages intentionally farming damage with no consequences.

---

## Smuggler Exploit Concern

No additional restrictions are planned.

The Smuggler is already limited by:

* 3% appearance rate
* Trophy loss on death
* Trophy loss on run abandonment
* 20% theft chance

---

## Room Rerolling

No additional protection is planned.

Starting a run already costs Energy.

Leaving early also causes trophy loss.

These costs are considered sufficient.

---

## Trophy Persistence

Trophies are lost when:

* The player dies
* The player abandons a run

This preserves the intended risk-versus-reward gameplay loop.

---

## Technical Risks

Technical concerns for Telegram Mini Apps will be evaluated during development and addressed progressively.

---

## Enemy Scaling

Enemies scale with player level.

Enemy Health and Damage increase as player level increases.

## Trap Room

When entering a Trap Room:

* An explosion immediately occurs.
* The player takes damage.
* Damage cannot be avoided.
* The player then proceeds to the next room.

---

Potions are purchased using Gold.

Each run begins with available potion charges if the player owns sufficient potions.

---

## Endurance Design Decision

Endurance scaling through taking damage is intentional and will remain unchanged.
